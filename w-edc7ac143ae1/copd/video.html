How it was made

Studio screens are unretouched captures from a running REALM-OS node, read as the notified body and the regulator; nothing was submitted. Terminal screens are real runs: the manufacturer's training script, the label check, the OMOP conversion, and the published model image served with networking disabled. Every output line shown is what the command printed.

Synchronisation is exact rather than estimated. The narration is assembled sentence by sentence, so every sentence boundary is a known audio offset, and those offsets drive the captions and the zooms.

Three disclosures, all also stated in the recording. The training data is a public teaching dataset of 101 patients whose clinical source is not documented, so this is a demonstration of the process, not evidence about COPD. The post-market cohorts and the twelve-month drift series in part eight are synthetic. And the voice is machine synthesised, by a neural text-to-speech model run locally.
