How it was made

The visuals are unretouched captures from a running REALM-OS node, taken through the notified-body lens. The highlight geometry was recorded in the same pass as the screenshots and normalised against each crop, so a box cannot drift from the figure it annotates.

Synchronisation is exact rather than estimated. The narration is assembled sentence by sentence, so every sentence boundary is a known audio offset, and those offsets drive both the captions and the zooms. The recording itself is played from a clock rather than from the audio element, and the soundtrack is the same audio the player uses, so picture and sound cannot drift apart over nine minutes.

Two disclosures, both also stated in the recording. The voice is machine synthesised, by a neural text-to-speech model run locally; the script is written to be read by a person and can be re-recorded without changing anything else. And the post-market surveillance dataset in chapter five is synthetic, while the model, the pre-market cohort of 16,829 patients and every computed figure are real.
